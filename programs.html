Our Programs

Nurturing focused minds and bright futures through structured learning and play.

Early Childhood Education & Care

We provide a safe, engaging environment tailored to different stages of your child's early development.

Day Care

A warm, home-like environment for our youngest stars. We focus on sensory play, basic motor skills, and providing the utmost care and attention while you are at work.

Nursery

Introducing the fundamentals of learning. Here, children engage in our beautiful Monday reading classes, basic counting, and interactive group activities with our best teachers.

Elementary

Preparing our pupils for their next big step. We focus on building strong foundations in literacy, numeracy, and independent thinking to ensure a seamless transition.

Beyond the Classroom

Education at Little Stars is holistic. We believe talent growth is a must!

  • Swimming: Fridays and weekends are for taking a dip and building physical confidence.
  • Concerts & Culture: High-priority events dedicated to talent discovery and joyous celebration.
  • Sports Days: Mingle in fun physical activities for both children and parents.
