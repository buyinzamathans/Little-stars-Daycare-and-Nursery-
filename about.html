About Little Stars

Welcome to Our Family

Located conveniently in Nansana-Wakiso, just near the business centre, Little Stars Nursery and Day Care is a place where life is fully experienced. We are deeply committed to providing a nurturing, safe, and vibrant environment for your little ones.

At Little Stars, we believe that education goes beyond the classroom. From our beautiful Monday reading classes with the best teachers to our energetic sports days, we ensure there is never a dull day in learning.
