About Little Stars

Focused minds, Bright futures!

Welcome to Our Family

Located conveniently in Nansana-Wakiso, just near the business centre, Little Stars Nursery and Day Care is a place where life is fully experienced. We are deeply committed to providing a safe, nurturing, and stimulating environment where every young learner can thrive, explore, and build a strong foundation for lifelong success.

At Little Stars, we believe that education goes beyond the classroom. From our beautiful Monday reading classes with the best teachers to our energetic sports days and swimming sessions, we ensure that every child balances physical activity with academic excellence. We care as you do!

Why Choose Us?

Building early childhood excellence through love, dedication, and play.

Talent Discovery

Talent growth is a must! Through our Concerts and Cultural Days, we make it a high priority to help every child discover and celebrate their unique gifts from an early age.

Dedicated Care

"We care as you do !!" Our qualified staff is passionate about early childhood development, treating every pupil with the warmth, love, and professional attention they deserve.

Holistic Growth

From weekend swimming sessions to interactive reading classes and dental health routines, we balance physical health, practical life skills, and academic foundational knowledge.
